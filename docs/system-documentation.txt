  GREEN STEEL AI NEWS GENERATION SYSTEM
  Technical Documentation & User Manual
  Version 1.0 | June 2026
================================================================================


OVERVIEW
--------
This system automatically generates professional news articles about the green
steel industry. It uses a six-stage AI pipeline powered by Anthropic Claude to
research live news, classify topics, write articles in wire-service journalism
style, and score them for quality before publishing.

The system covers 15 industry categories — from renewable energy and hydrogen
production to trade policy and project finance — and produces output in three
formats: PDF, Markdown, and JSON.


================================================================================
HOW THE PIPELINE WORKS
================================================================================

When you enter a topic and click Generate, the system runs six agents in
sequence. Each agent does one job and passes its output to the next.


  [User Input]
       |
       v
  [1. Research Agent]  ──>  fetches live news articles (2026 only)
       |
       v
  [2. Category Agent]  ──>  classifies the topic into 1 of 15 categories
       |
       v
  [3. Writer Agent]    ──>  writes a 500-700 word draft article
       |
       v
  [4. Humanizer Agent] ──>  rewrites to wire-service journalism style
       |
       v
  [5. Quality Agent]   ──>  scores the article 0-10 on five dimensions
       |
       v
  [6. Output]          ──>  saves to News Feed, available as PDF/MD/JSON


================================================================================
AGENT-BY-AGENT BREAKDOWN
================================================================================


AGENT 1 — RESEARCH AGENT
--------------------------
Purpose: Find real, recent news articles about the topic.

How it works:
  The agent tries three data sources in order (fallback chain):

  Tier 1 — NewsAPI (https://newsapi.org)
    - Requires a NEWS_API_KEY set in the .env file
    - Searches for the topic combined with category-specific keywords
    - Filters to articles published on or after 1 January 2026
    - Returns up to 20 articles sorted by publication date

  Tier 2 — Google News RSS (https://news.google.com/rss/search)
    - Free, no API key required
    - Always runs alongside NewsAPI as a supplement
    - Searches two queries: "{topic} green steel" and "{category terms} 2026"
    - Returns up to 15 articles per query

  Tier 3 — Category RSS Feeds
    - Runs only if fewer than 8 articles have been gathered from Tiers 1 and 2
    - Uses curated RSS feeds per category (e.g. hydrogeninsight.com,
      steelwatch.org, cleantechnica.com, mining.com, carbonbrief.org)
    - Returns up to 8 articles per feed

  After gathering, the agent:
    - Filters out any article published before 1 January 2026
    - Removes duplicate headlines
    - Scores each article for relevance (keyword match + topic match + recency)
    - Selects the top 15 articles
    - Labels each article with its age: "today", "3 days ago", "[OLDER SOURCE]"

  The agent then sends the labelled articles to Claude with strict instructions:
    - Use ONLY facts from articles dated "today", "yesterday", or "X days ago"
    - Facts from [OLDER SOURCE] articles may only be used as background context
    - Do not invent any figure not present in the articles
    - Return: topic, facts[], sources[], suggested_angle, key_players[]

Data sources used:
  - NewsAPI:           https://newsapi.org/v2/everything
  - Google News RSS:   https://news.google.com/rss/search
  - Hydrogen Insight:  https://www.hydrogeninsight.com/rss
  - SteelWatch:        https://steelwatch.org/feed/
  - CleanTechnica:     https://cleantechnica.com/feed/
  - Carbon Brief:      https://carbonbrief.org/feed
  - Mining.com:        https://www.mining.com/feed/
  - World Steel Assoc: https://www.worldsteel.org/rss.xml
  - Recycling Today:   https://www.recyclingtoday.com/rss/all-news.rss
  - Fuel Cell Works:   https://www.fuelcellsworks.com/feed/


AGENT 2 — CATEGORY AGENT
--------------------------
Purpose: Classify the topic into exactly one of 15 industry categories.

The 15 categories are:
   1.  Renewable Energy
   2.  Hydrogen Production & Technology
   3.  Green Iron & Low-Carbon Feedstocks
   4.  Circular Economy (Scrap)
   5.  CCS & CCUS
   6.  Steel Demand, Procurement & End Markets
   7.  Steel Prices & Green Premiums
   8.  Raw Material Prices
   9.  Clean Energy Logistics & Storage
  10.  Project Finance & Investment
  11.  Trade, Tariffs & Regulations
  12.  Climate Policy & Environment
  13.  Corporate Offtake
  14.  Partnerships & M&A
  15.  Green Steel Projects & Plant Development

How it works:
  - Sends the research brief (topic, facts, key players, suggested angle)
    to Claude with the list of 15 categories
  - Claude returns: category name, confidence score (0.0-1.0), one-sentence
    reasoning explaining why that category fits
  - If the user selects a category override in the UI, the LLM call is skipped
    and that category is used directly with confidence 1.0


AGENT 3 — WRITER AGENT
------------------------
Purpose: Write a first draft of the news article.

How it works:
  - Receives the research brief and the assigned category
  - Each category has its own writing guidance specifying what the lead must
    contain. Examples:
      * Renewable Energy: must include MW/GW figure, named counterparties,
        contract duration, link to steel or hydrogen output
      * Project Finance: must lead with capital amount, break down
        equity/debt/grant, name lead investors and financial close date
      * Green Steel Projects: must include capacity (Mt/yr), technology route
        (H2-DRI, EAF, MOE), location, CAPEX, first-production date
  - Writer rules (non-negotiable):
      * First sentence is the news event — not background, not context
      * Every paragraph must have at least one named company and one number
      * At least one sentence under 9 words; at least one over 30 words
      * Closing paragraph is always a direct quote
      * 500-700 words
      * No bullet points, no subheadings, no bold text in paragraphs
  - Format: Headline / Dateline / 4-5 body paragraphs / closing quote

Style target: Metal Bulletin, Argus Metals, Kallanish Steel


AGENT 4 — HUMANIZER AGENT
---------------------------
Purpose: Rewrite the draft to eliminate AI writing patterns.

How it works:
  - Acts as a veteran wire-service editor (Reuters Commodities desk)
  - Applies a kill list of banned AI phrases:
      significant, notably, importantly, landscape, leverage, robust,
      game-changer, paradigm, milestone, paving the way, underscores,
      showcases, furthermore, moreover, it is worth noting, transformative,
      unprecedented, exciting, in today's world, going forward
  - Structural rules:
      * Never open a paragraph with a gerund (-ing word)
      * No paragraph starting with "The company / The project / The deal"
        three times in a row
      * Vary sentence length — one sentence under 8 words, one over 35 words
      * Paragraphs: 1 to 5 sentences, not all the same length
  - Numbers: every number from the draft must appear in the rewrite
  - Quotes: format must be — "Quote text," said Firstname Lastname,
    Title at Organisation
  - The closing quote must be a judgement or reaction, not a press-release line
  - Returns only the rewritten article — no commentary


AGENT 5 — QUALITY AGENT
-------------------------
Purpose: Score the article before it is published.

Five dimensions scored 0-10:

  Newsworthiness  — Is there a specific recent event? Score 0 if it reads
                    like background analysis.

  Specificity     — Are there concrete figures (amounts, %, dates)?
                    Below 5 if any paragraph has no number or named company.

  Readability     — Does it read like wire-service journalism or an AI report?
                    Deduct 2 points per AI filler phrase found.

  Structure       — Does it open with the sharpest fact?
                    Is the closing a real quote?
                    Deduct points if it opens with context or background.

  Category Fit    — Does the dominant content match the assigned category?

Overall score = average of the five dimensions.
Pass threshold = 7.0

  Score 0-4:  Unacceptable — would not publish
  Score 5-6:  Needs significant rewrite
  Score 7-8:  Publishable with minor edits
  Score 9-10: Publish as-is

If the score is below 7.0, the agent returns specific feedback: what is wrong
and exactly how to fix it.


AGENT 6 — OUTPUT / STORAGE
----------------------------
Purpose: Save the article and make it available for download.

The article is stored in Streamlit session state and displayed in the News Feed
tab. Each article card shows:
  - Headline and dateline
  - Category badge (colour-coded per category)
  - Quality badge: PASS (green) or FAIL (red) with overall score
  - Full article body
  - Sources list
  - Download buttons: PDF | Markdown | JSON

Bulk download: if multiple articles are generated (batch or sweep mode),
a "Download All as ZIP" button packages all articles in all three formats.


================================================================================
GENERATION MODES
================================================================================

SINGLE ARTICLE
  Enter one topic. Click Generate Article.
  The full 6-agent pipeline runs for that topic.
  Time: approximately 60-90 seconds.

BATCH TOPICS
  Enter multiple topics, one per line.
  Click Generate Batch.
  Each topic runs through the full pipeline sequentially.
  Results appear in the News Feed as they complete.

CATEGORY SWEEP
  Click Run Category Sweep.
  The system generates one article per category (15 total) using a
  pre-set topic relevant to that category.
  Useful for producing a full edition covering all industry segments.
  Time: approximately 15-20 minutes.


================================================================================
OUTPUT FORMATS
================================================================================

PDF
  Generated using ReportLab (Python library).
  Contains: headline, dateline, category, quality score, body text, sources.
  Unicode-safe — handles em dashes, euro signs, and special characters.

MARKDOWN (.md)
  Plain text with Markdown formatting.
  Contains: headline, metadata block, full body, sources list.
  Suitable for CMS import or documentation systems.

JSON (.json)
  Machine-readable structured data.
  Contains: topic, category, quality scores (all 5 dimensions), article text,
  sources list, key players, timestamp.
  Suitable for database ingestion or API responses.
